At 3:17 a.m., the moon vanished from the sky without warning, and every clock on Earth stopped at the exact same second. As scientists scrambled for answers, a retired subway conductor in New York received a mysterious text message that simply read, "Do not let them open the seventh door." Before he could tell anyone, a glowing staircase appeared in the middle of Times Square and began descending deep beneath the city. Thousands watched in silence as something started climbing up from the darkness!!
